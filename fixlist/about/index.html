Fixlist: snag & punch lists

An Android app for recording snags, punch-list items and defects on site, then sending a professional PDF or Excel report before you leave.

What it does

  • Unlimited photos per item, with before and after photos and mark-up
  • Floors and rooms, floor plans with pins, status, priority, trade and due dates
  • PDF reports with your logo and signature, plus Excel and CSV
  • Works offline and saves as you type; no account needed
  • Share project files with colleagues and merge their changes
  • Back up to your own Google Drive, or to a file in OneDrive, Dropbox or anywhere else

Price

7-day full trial, then a single in-app purchase. No subscription. Your projects always stay yours to view and export.

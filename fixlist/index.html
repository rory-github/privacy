Privacy policy

Fixlist: Snag & Punch Lists
Last updated 20 November 2026

How we handle information when you use Fixlist, an app for recording snags, punch-list items and defects.

Who we are

Monolith Industries Ltd (company number 17555911) provides this app. Contact help@monolithindustries.co.uk about this policy or your information.

Your projects stay on your device

Projects, items, photos, mark-up, floor plans, notes, your company details, logo and signature are stored on your device. There is no Fixlist account and we do not run servers that receive your projects. Reports, spreadsheets and project files are created on your device.

Camera, photos and location

The app uses the camera only when you take a photo, and reads photos from your gallery only when you choose them. If you turn on “Location on photos” for a project, the app records where each photo was taken and stores it with the item on your device. This is off by default and can be turned off per project. If you turn on copying photos to the gallery, copies are saved in Pictures/Fixlist on your device.

Dictation

When you use the microphone button, your speech is handled by the speech recognition service on your device (for example Google’s). Network use depends on that service and your settings. Fixlist only receives the resulting text.

Sharing reports and project files

When you share a report, spreadsheet, project file or backup file, it goes only to the app or storage you choose, such as email, a messaging app, Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox. Those services apply their own privacy policies.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you turn on Google Drive backup, the app asks your permission to use a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive. Your projects, photos, plans and company details are uploaded there. The app can only see files it created in that folder, not the rest of your Drive. We do not receive a copy. You can turn backup off in the app, and remove the stored data from Google Drive’s settings (Manage apps). Google applies its own privacy policy.

Android backups

Android may back up your projects and settings to your Google account, or transfer them to a new device, depending on your settings. Google or your device provider manages that service. Photos are included when moving directly to a new device. You can manage backups through Android settings.

Crash reports

To find and fix crashes, the app uses Firebase Crashlytics, a Google service. When the app crashes, it sends technical information: the app version, device model, Android version, the error and the code that failed, and a random installation identifier. Crash reports do not include your projects, photos or notes. We use this information, based on our legitimate interest in keeping the app reliable. Google keeps crash reports for up to 90 days.

Purchases

Google Play processes purchases. The app uses purchase information, such as the product and purchase token, to verify and restore access, and keeps confirmed access on the device for offline use. The 7-day trial is tracked on your device. We do not receive your card details. Google applies its own privacy policy to its services.

When you email us

We receive your email address, message and any information you choose to include. Cloudflare Email Routing forwards support email to our Google email account. We use this information to answer your enquiry and support the app, based on our legitimate interests in providing customer support. We may also keep records where required by law.

Retention and deletion

Your projects remain on your device until you delete them or remove the app. Deleting a project removes its photos and plans from the app’s storage. Copies you have shared, saved to backup files, or copied to the gallery are not removed by the app. Google Drive backup copies stay until you delete them. We retain support correspondence while handling your enquiry and for as long as reasonably needed for follow-up or legal obligations. You can contact us to request deletion.

Your choices and rights

There is no advertising in this app and no analytics beyond crash reports. We do not sell your information. Depending on the circumstances, you can ask to access, correct or delete personal information we hold, or object to or restrict its use. Email help@monolithindustries.co.uk to make a request. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Information about other people

Your projects may contain information about clients, contractors or homeowners, such as names and addresses. You control that information and decide whom to share reports with. Please share it only as your own obligations allow.

Children

Fixlist is a work tool and is not directed at children.

Updates

We will update this policy if the app or our information handling changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.

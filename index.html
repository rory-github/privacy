How we handle information when you use Driving Theory Test 5 in 1 Kit.

Who we are

Monolith Industries Ltd (company number 17555911) provides this app. Contact help@monolithindustries.co.uk about this policy or your information.

Your revision stays on your device

The app stores your answers, saved questions, scores, practice history, trial dates and purchase status locally. There is no app account and we do not upload your revision history to our own servers. Suggested practice is calculated on your device.

Android backups

Android may back up your progress and trial information or transfer them to another device, depending on your settings. Google or your device provider manages that service. Backup restoration is not guaranteed. You can manage backups through Android settings.

Purchases

Google Play processes purchases. The app uses purchase information, such as the product and purchase token, to verify and restore access, and keeps confirmed access on the device for offline use. We do not receive your card details. Google applies its own privacy policy to its services.

Read aloud

When you use read aloud, the selected text is passed to the text-to-speech service on your device. Voice setup and any network use depend on your installed speech provider and settings.

When you email us

We receive your email address, message and any information you choose to include. Cloudflare Email Routing forwards support email to our Google email account. We use this information to answer your enquiry and support the app, based on our legitimate interests in providing customer support. We may also keep records where required by law.

Retention and deletion

Local revision data remains until you remove it or it is replaced through a restore. Clearing app storage can remove it; Android backup copies are managed separately. We retain support correspondence while handling your enquiry and for as long as reasonably needed for follow-up or legal obligations. You can contact us to request deletion.

Your choices and rights

There are no advertising or analytics services in this app. We do not sell your information. Depending on the circumstances, you can ask to access, correct or delete personal information we hold, or object to or restrict its use. Email help@monolithindustries.co.uk to make a request. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Pass guarantee claims

Preparation records are stored on your device and may be included in Android backups. If you choose to prepare a claim, the app asks for your test date, includes it in a PDF report and shares the report with your chosen email app. We receive the report, purchase receipt and failed-test result only when you email them. We use them to assess eligibility, prevent duplicate guarantee refunds and refund the app purchase through Google Play while preserving full app access. Claims are reviewed manually; reports contain local practice evidence, not question text or videos. We retain claim correspondence and refund records for as long as reasonably needed for administration, fraud prevention and legal obligations. You can contact us about your information.

Updates

We will update this policy if the app or our information handling changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
